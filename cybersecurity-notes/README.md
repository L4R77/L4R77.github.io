## Principles

- **Clear** — Notes should be understandable months later.
- **Practical** — Include commands, examples and use cases.
- **Referenced** — Link to official documentation when possible.
- **Living** — Notes are updated as knowledge grows.

---

## What Belongs Here

✅ Technical notes and references.

✅ Command cheatsheets and examples.

✅ Configuration templates.

✅ Best practices and procedures.

✅ Links to official documentation.

---

## What Does NOT Belong Here

❌ Real credentials, tokens or secrets.

❌ Proprietary information from employers.

❌ Unauthorized documentation.

❌ Personal opinions without technical basis.

❌ Copied content without attribution.

---

## Security

- No sensitive data is stored in this repository.
- All notes are for personal reference and learning.
- Review content before committing to ensure no accidental
  inclusion of sensitive information.
